# Music Disc Maker

Craft playable music discs from YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, internet radio, and direct audio links. You can also import a local MP3 and share it through your server.

[Watch the demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7sUJqdRrS3M)

Music Disc Maker turns a Blank Disc and a URL or imported audio file into a Custom Music Disc. Play it in a vanilla jukebox with positional audio and the vanilla “Now Playing” overlay, or use the added players and displays for larger builds.

## Features

- Custom discs with track title, artist, length, source, and cover art in the tooltip
- Albums that hold up to 18 custom discs in two rows, list their contents, and keep their contents and order when dyed
- Import an MP3 from your computer; the server stores the audio so players on the server can listen to the shared disc
- A portable or placeable Boombox with play/pause, previous/next, shuffle, repeat, seek, and volume controls
- Optional Curios slot for an equipped Boombox on supported NeoForge and Forge versions
- A Golden Jukebox with 16–256 block range, 0–200% volume, positional or flat audio, Album controls, and resource-pack support for its sliders, playback bar, and selected device model parts
- A Minecart with Golden Jukebox for playing and controlling music on rails
- Speakers that link to a Golden Jukebox, mount on floors, walls, ceilings, or fence posts, and have individual volume and redstone mute
- A Disc Dyeing Table for separate surface and accent colors
- A Disc Pedestal that shows one custom disc with its track title and artist
- Internet radio and YouTube live discs with automatic reconnection
- Up to 64 simultaneous custom tracks without changing settings; the limit adjusts on devices with fewer available audio channels

## Basic use

1. Craft a Blank Disc with lapis lazuli and iron, then craft a Music Disc Maker.
2. Open the Music Disc Maker, paste a supported track URL or choose a local MP3, and insert the Blank Disc.
3. Confirm the displayed title, then take the Custom Music Disc from the output. Play it in a vanilla jukebox, Golden Jukebox, or Boombox.

URL audio streams from their source each time they play, so URL playback needs an internet connection. Imported audio is shared by the server. Use the same Music Disc Maker version on the server and every client.

## Compatibility

- Additional Additions Albums and Pocket Jukebox
- Sophisticated Backpacks jukebox upgrade
- Traveler's Backpack jukebox upgrade
- Create moving contraptions on NeoForge 1.21.1 and Forge 1.20.1; Create Aeronautics physics airships on NeoForge 1.21.1

- Optional Curios Boombox slot on NeoForge 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, and 26.2, and Forge 1.20.1. Curios is not needed for other features.

### Resource-pack models

Resource packs can replace the Speaker horn models and the Boombox handle with standard Minecraft model JSON, then adjust their animation pivots. The [resource-pack model guide](./_docs/RESOURCE_PACK_SHAPES_3.1.0.md) includes the file paths and a small JSON example. These model changes affect appearance; they do not change collision, selection, or sound direction.
