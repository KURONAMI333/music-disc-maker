## Make a disc

Craft a **Music Disc Maker**, then make a **Blank Disc** with lapis lazuli and iron. Open the maker, paste a track URL or choose an audio or video file from your computer, and insert the blank disc. Confirm the displayed title, then take your finished **Custom Music Disc** and play it in an ordinary jukebox.

Use links from **YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp**, or a direct audio URL. Discs show the track title, artist, duration, source, and cover art in their tooltip. You can also rename them on an anvil. Imported audio is stored by the server so other players on that server can hear the shared disc; the selected file path is not shared.

## Build your collection

- **Album:** Keep up to 18 discs together in two rows, whether they are custom discs, vanilla discs, or music discs from other mods. Right-click to arrange them, check the contents in its tooltip, and dye the cover to tell your collections apart. Dyeing preserves the discs, their order, and the Album's name.
- **Golden Album:** Surround an Album with 8 gold ingots for 54 slots in six rows, the size of a large chest. The discs, their order, the color, and the name carry over.
- **Disc Dyeing Table:** Recolor a custom disc's surface and accent separately. Change either color or both.
- **Disc Pedestal:** Put a favorite disc on display with its track title and artist. Swap the displayed disc in one interaction.

## Listen at home or on the move

The **Boombox** plays any music disc that works in a jukebox, including vanilla discs and discs from other mods, or an Album, while carried or placed. Pause, skip between tracks, shuffle an Album, repeat, seek within a song, and adjust the volume from its controls. On supported NeoForge and Forge versions, an optional Curios slot lets you equip a Boombox. Curios is not needed for the rest of the mod.

The **Golden Jukebox** gives you control over a listening area: set the range from **4 to 256 blocks**, adjust the volume from **0 to 200%**, and choose positional sound or an even volume throughout the area. It also has playback controls, including track skipping and shuffle for Albums, and each redstone pulse starts or pauses the music.

The **Minecart with Golden Jukebox** brings playback controls to a rail build. Insert a compatible disc or Album, then open its controls while it is stopped or moving.

Link **Speakers** to a Golden Jukebox to bring its music to other rooms or parts of a build. Mount them on floors, walls, ceilings, or fence posts, and turn each horn straight, left, or right. Each Speaker has its own volume; the jukebox sets their range. Apply a redstone signal to mute a Speaker.

## Resource-pack models

Resource packs can replace the Speaker horn and Boombox handle models with standard Minecraft model JSON and adjust the animated parts' pivots. See the [resource-pack model guide](https://github.com/KURONAMI333/music-disc-maker/blob/master/_docs/RESOURCE_PACK_SHAPES_3.1.0.md) for the paths and example JSON. These model changes affect appearance, not collision, selection, or sound direction.

## Radio and shared listening

Icecast/SHOUTcast streams and YouTube live links become **live radio discs**. They show LIVE instead of a track length and reconnect after brief interruptions.

On servers, playback is synchronized so players joining an ongoing track hear the same part. Play **up to 64 custom tracks at once** without changing settings; devices with fewer available audio channels use a lower limit automatically.

## Compatibility

- **Additional Additions:** Custom discs work in Albums and the Pocket Jukebox.
- **Sophisticated Backpacks** and **Traveler's Backpack:** Custom discs work with their jukebox upgrades.
- **BiblioCraft Legacy:** Custom discs fit its disc racks on 1.21.1.
- **[Let's Do] Furniture:** Custom discs play in the Gramophone on 1.20.1 and 1.21.1.
- **Create: Steam 'n' Rails:** Custom discs play in the Minecart with Jukebox on 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1.
- **Create:** Golden Jukebox audio follows moving contraptions on NeoForge 1.21.1 and Forge 1.20.1. Create Aeronautics airships are supported on NeoForge 1.21.1.
- **Valkyrien Skies 2, Eureka!, and Clockwork:** Audio follows ships on 1.20.1.
- **Curios:** Optional Boombox slot on NeoForge 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, and 26.2, and Forge 1.20.1. Not supported or required on Fabric.

## Before you play

URL audio streams from their source rather than being stored inside the disc, so URL playback needs an internet connection. Imported audio is stored by the server for shared playback. Spotify links use the song and artist to find a matching track on YouTube. Some tracks may be unavailable, region-locked, or blocked by a login or bot check. Try a direct audio link, SoundCloud, or Bandcamp if that happens. Twitch is not supported.

Use the same Music Disc Maker version on the server and every client.
